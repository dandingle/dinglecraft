Weapons: craft pistols, shotguns, SMGs and sniper rifles from iron/gold/diamond + sticks at a Crafting Table (the sniper scope needs glass). Bullets = 1 iron + 1 gunpowder (8) · Shells = 1 iron + 2 gunpowder (4) · Grenades = 3 gunpowder + 1 iron (2). Gold guns shoot faster but wear out quickly; diamond hits hardest and lasts.
Cars: craft a Wheel from 4 iron in a diamond shape, then a Car from 3 iron + 2 iron + glass + 2 wheels. Right-click the ground with the car item to park it. Cars hop up one-block ledges at speed, take crash damage, and drop themselves when broken — punch a parked car to pack it back up.
Torches now sit on floors or mount on walls — break the supporting block and they pop off.
Sort inventory R (with inventory open)
Powers menu P  ·  Laser Eyes Sneak + Right-click, empty hand
Boat Right-click water to launch · W/S throttle · A/D steer · Shift hop out
Dragons Feed 5 steak to tame (don't hit it first) · Right-click bare-handed to ride · fly where you look, Space climbs, Shift dismounts
Armor: craft helmets, chestplates, leggings and boots from iron, gold or diamond (dragon scales make the best set). Right-click to wear — the ⛨ chip shows your protection. Pieces wear out and shatter; swap by right-clicking another piece.
Water now flows: dig a channel from the sea, flood your enemies, build waterfalls. Swim against a bank and jump to clamber out. A surfing skateboard can ollie clean out of the water.
Buried Dungeons & Vaults: ruined stone stairways on the surface lead down to sprawling strongholds — spawner halls, loot chests, and a Warden at the end. Smaller sealed spawner vaults hide in the deep rock for miners to crack open. Your fallen items wait where you died: follow the † marker by the coordinates.
The DINGLE STORE™: convert diamonds into DingleBucks at a rate economists call "predatory", then spend them on hats (rendered live in third person, F), particle trails, loot boxes containing dirt, a Battle Pass that pays in dirt, and the legendary PAY-TO-WIN SWORD (wooden). Find it in the pause menu. No refunds.
Superpowers: Super Jump, Swift Feet, Feather Fall, Fire Heart, Laser Eyes and Sky Walker (survival flight — double-tap Space). Unlock with Power Crystals, toggle from the P menu.
Game rules: pick them on Create New World (Keep Inventory, Mob Griefing, Peaceful, Fire Tick, the Eternal Snail, Guilt Ghosts, the Fullbright toggle, the Dingle Store, Superpowers, Cheats, AI Players). Right-click a saved world on the title to change any of them later. With Cheats on, Settings also shows the Debug Menu: the gamemode switch, God Mode, Fall Damage, a Daylight Cycle pause and a slider that drags the sun across the sky. Rules save with the world; your settings are remembered between sessions.
Spawn Eggs & Nuke Kegs: creative mode now stocks a spawn egg for every mob in the game — pigs to MALGORATH himself. Right-click to place. The two new green ones are Nuke Kegs (walking gunpowder barrels, or the armed variant): they hunt you even in creative, flash white, swell, and then level the neighbourhood with a mushroom cloud you can feel. You were warned.
Structure Compass: craft from 4 iron around a diamond, then right-click to tune it to any structure — Alien Villages, Theme Parks, Buried Dungeons, Spawner Vaults, Dragon Roosts, or Malgorath's Bite. While held, a needle above the hotbar points at the nearest one and counts down the metres.
The Final Boss: MALGORATH has been rebuilt from the bones up. He is at X 1000, Z 1000. Bring everything. Slaying him ends the story — and the world keeps going.
Boss Fights: the Dragon King rules gold-strewn roosts on the peaks — fell him for the free Dragon Crown and a fistful of crystals. Or craft a Titan Core (stone bricks + gold around a diamond) and place it, if you think you can take what wakes up. The Warden still guards every dungeon. All bosses drop Power Crystals.
Critter Jars: craft from 4 iron around glass (makes 2). A thrown jar that hits any mob traps it — pick the jar up and the badge shows what's inside. Throwing a filled jar releases the mob at full stored health and the empty jar pops back out for reuse.
Skateboard: craft from 3 planks over 2 wheels. Speed carries — pump it with W, ollie up ledges, spin in the air for a landing boost, and grind any block edge or 1-wide rail (sparks fly, speed holds). Hopping out of a grind gives a little pop. Wipe out into a wall above ~8 speed and you'll bail. Drops convert into roll speed, so hunt for hills.
New this update: healing is much quicker on a fed stomach. Skateboards become surfboards the instant they touch water — ride straight off the beach — and nudge along the ground when you walk into them. Wooden ramps (6 planks → 4) launch boards, cars and even sprinting players; chain ramps, spins and grinds and a live trick score tallies overhead, banking on a clean landing and vanishing on a bail. Vehicles now place facing away from you and you board them facing forward.
Beds & brighter nights: craft a bed from 3 wool over 3 planks. Right-click it any time to set your respawn point; right-click at night to sleep straight through to morning (and heal a heart). Nights themselves are now moonlit rather than pitch black — and if you want to see everything in a cave or dungeon, flip Fullbright in the pause menu for full night-vision brightness.
The Dungeon Egg: craft it from 8 diamonds around glass, crack it, and a vast stone-brick dungeon materialises around you — an entry hall, three spawner rooms pouring out zombies and skeletons, side vaults, loot chests of iron, diamonds, guns and rarities, and at the end of the long corridor: THE DUNGEON WARDEN. Six hearts a hit and it launches you skyward, but fell it and it drops a diamond tool already carrying powerful random enchantments, plus a fountain of XP.
Enchanting: mobs now drop glowing XP orbs. Craft an Enchantment Table (glass + 3 diamonds + stone) and right-click it while holding a tool, sword or bow: pick from three offered enchants — Sharpness, Lifesteal, Knockback, Efficiency, Fortune, Power, Unbreaking. Enchant the same item again to raise levels or stack new effects; the price climbs with every rune already on it. Break a mob spawner for a 15 XP bounty.
Theme parks & coasters: wooden rollercoasters now dot the world — a full circuit with a chain-lift hill, a big drop, bumps, a station, ticket hut, snack stall and torch-lit grounds. Hop in a cart (right-click), hold W to roll out of the station and the lift does the rest. Build your own with Coaster Rails (iron + stick → 12), Sloped Rails (rail atop a ramp) and Carts (5 iron) — carts follow corners, climb slowly on lift hills, scream down drops, and fly off open ends. Cars also now keep full speed when climbing blocks.
Disasters, rebalanced for the apocalypse: the sphere now lets you CHOOSE your disaster — and every one is far deadlier, with permanent scars. Tornadoes tear up soft terrain itself; tsunamis permanently raise the local sea six blocks; the volcano is nearly twice the size, erupts for two minutes and its lava never cools; fire rain burns planks and wool too, leaving scorched dirt behind. Choose wisely. Or don't.
Alien villages: green-skinned settlers from Dingl-7 have crash-landed across the world — look for a glowing saucer ringed by dome huts, or summon one anywhere with a Village Egg (8 iron in a ring). Right-click a villager to chat: proper dialogue trees with lore, gifts (they adore diamonds), flirting and — if you charm them enough — a partnership proposal with a gem to seal it. Or go the other way: keep insulting one and the entire village turns hostile. They calm down after a minute or so, if you survive. Relationships, names and grudges all persist in your save.
The Dingle Casino: craft one from 8 gold around a diamond. It shares your exchange balance — deposit diamonds, then feed the slots (triples pay up to 30×, pairs refund) or sit down for blackjack with hit, stand and double. The exchange now also shows whether each of your holdings is up or down, by how many diamonds and what percent, plus your overall P/L — so you know exactly what you can afford to lose at the tables.
The Dingle Exchange: craft a Computer (6 iron, glass screen, diamond chip) and right-click it to trade made-up stocks with diamonds. Deposit gems into your balance, buy fractions, and watch live charts tick up and down in the background whether or not the screen is open. Sell high, withdraw whole diamonds, and keep the loose change invested.
Disaster Spheres: craft from 4 diamonds around gunpowder. Smashing one summons a random local catastrophe for 2–3 minutes: a tornado that shreds trees and flings debris, a tsunami that floods the area then recedes, fire rain that ignites forests, or a volcano that erupts beneath you — you're lifted to the rim while a one-minute fuse counts down, then lava bombs and flows pour out (they cool to stone when it ends). Disasters never happen on their own. Cars can also climb single blocks now, and lava hurts — a lot.
Doors: 6 planks → 3 doors. They face you when placed, swing open with a click, and pop off if the floor under them is broken.
Saving: worlds now persist in your browser even when this file is opened locally, and the title screen offers a one-click Continue into the last world you played (imports included).
Touch: left stick = move · drag right side = look · quick tap = place/use · press & hold = mine/attack · JUMP double-tap = fly (creative).
In menus: click = pick up/place stack · right-click = half/one · shift-click = quick move · shift-click result = craft all. Open a Crafting Table and press Recipes to browse and auto-fill any recipe you have materials for.
Getting started: punch a tree → logs → planks → sticks → crafting table → wooden pickaxe → cobblestone → stone tools → furnace. Coal or wood fuels the furnace. Torches stop monsters spawning nearby. Beware green hissing things.
Saving: worlds auto-save every 60s and on quit, in this browser (clearing your browsing data deletes them). Export World (pause menu, under Save World) downloads a .vxw.json you can re-import from Load World — the save format (v1) is versioned and will keep working in future game versions.
🔥 The Nether: build a 4x5 obsidian frame (water + lava makes obsidian) and light it with a torch. Fortresses, imps, INFERNIS. Bring armor.
☁️ The Aether: build the frame from glowstone (found in the Nether) and pour a water bucket into it. Sky islands, shrines, VALKYRA. Falling off returns you to the overworld — eventually.
🧰 Gadgets: ten of them, hiding in dimension dungeon chests and boss drops. Passives (boots, glider, magnet, claws, trampoline, belt) work from your inventory; actives (blink pearl, grappling anchor, pig cannon) fire on right-click while held. Gadget and skateboard models: see Credits below.
Armor slots: open your inventory to see all four equipped pieces and swap them by clicking. Armor now shows on your character in third person (F5).
DEEP DIRT 2D: buy the Gaming Rig in the DINGLE STORE, place it, right-click to play a 2D mining game. WASD/Space to move, LMB mine/fight, RMB build, 1-6 select, C to craft, Esc saves and quits. The save lives inside that specific rig.
Subscribe Button: DINGLE STORE™ exclusive (1287 DB). Right-click to throw — it boomerangs back, smacking mobs both ways and fetching dropped items it touches. Enchant with Viral, Recoil and Unbreaking.
3D weapons & icons: guns and the Bow (a crossbow, don't ask) are real 3D models too, and every tool/weapon icon is rendered from its actual model.
⚙️ Settings & ✨ Shaders: the pause menu's Settings panel holds the UI scale, render distance, field of view, your name, mouse sensitivity, the sound and music volume sliders (music covers the menu music and records), Sound and World Music switches, fullbright, the texture pack, mouse capture and a Shaders toggle for bloom/vignette/colour-grade pretty-mode (plus the Debug Menu when Cheats are on).
3D tools: pickaxes, axes, shovels and swords are now real 3D models in your hand (and in F5 view, and on the ground). Free CC0 models (Kenney, KayKit), lovingly adopted.
